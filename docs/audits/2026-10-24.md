its `INVALID_QUERY` error) and `NOT_FOUND`." (This mirrors the command-client's Purpose, whose four codes all genuinely
appear in the command-service spec.)

Kafka, OpenSearch) single-sourced: … the deployment pins the Bitnami Helm chart versions …". The "Infrastructure image
references are single-sourced" requirement (lines 14-24) also governs the observability charts'
(`prometheus-community-stack`, `grafana-tempo`) and the application chart's `common` subchart concrete-version pinning,
