**2. Normative SHALL paragraph detached after the scenarios of "Review-round rows record the reviewer session's
continuity"**

- **Anchor**: `openspec/specs/showcase/quality/commit-hygiene/spec.md:799-801`
- **Evidence**: the paragraph
  `` `caller-restarted` SHALL record a continuity violation, not an additional permitted recovery case; the verification SHALL validate the recorded fields, and passing it SHALL NOT attest that the stated cause occurred or authorize the review gate to continue. ``
  sits after the requirement's final scenario (792–797), breaking the body-then-scenarios convention — a SHALL
  obligation stranded below the WHEN/THEN scenarios of its own requirement.
- **Rewrite**: move lines 799–801 into the requirement body, immediately after the body paragraph ending at line 697. On
  relocation, drop the first clause ("a continuity violation, not an additional permitted recovery case"), which the
  body already states verbatim ("`caller-restarted` for a restart already performed without an evidenced recovery case
  (a violation record, not a recovery case)"), and retain only the obligations the body does not yet carry: "the
  verification SHALL validate the recorded fields, and passing it SHALL NOT attest that the stated cause occurred or
  authorize the review gate to continue."
