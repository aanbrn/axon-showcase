## Lessons carried through the window

The window's captures are overwhelmingly mechanism lessons: claims seeded from the diff (#609), the canonical class
list, session continuity, the contiguity net, the selective capture gate (#626), the authoring-system routing (#618),
the extraction procedure with its per-obligation trace (#645), and the deliberate decision to keep the retrospective out
of the scheduled audit.

## Went well

- **The loaded surface shrank measurably.** `AGENTS.md` was 3,101 lines at #592's parent and is 2,596 at `HEAD` (−505,
  ≈16.3%); four on-demand subject packs and 19 retargeted `captured:` markers came with it. The maintained corpus did
  not shrink — bullets rose 172 → 220, and the packs plus `docs/guidance-evidence/` grew — so the measured win is the
  always-loaded surface, not less guidance to maintain.
- **The window's own net caught this session's two late appends** in the same turn, through the contiguity checkpoint
  (#658); the second was recorded as `already covered` rather than turned into a fourth rule about the same act. The
  wider record is in `2026-10-22-check-review-round-contiguity/design.md`, which counts five late appends across two
  earlier units and the rounds that reported them.
- **The gate consolidation produced one owner for the procedure.** `.opencode/skills/review-gate/SKILL.md` carries it in
  209 lines and the six split requirements state outcomes: the procedure has one operational owner, while the mandatory
  related updates (the delta and the docs sweep) remain.
- **Both cases' scoping decisions were shaped by consultation**, as the session records them — the per-obligation trace,
  the narrow `review-gate` retarget in the capture destinations, the caller-decision fixtures, the parked-gap record.
  Whether that is cheaper than drafting and reviewing without it is not established; the second case's 9 rounds are
  consistent with it, and so are the first case's 25.
- **`technical-advisor` plus `consult-terminal-handover` closed a hazard** — a consultation's closing prompt applied
  before the owner decided — by making the pass end the turn.

## Went wrong

- **The record's absolute rework is the window's largest so far**: 1,169 recorded compliance misses across 701 rounds.
  Its per-round density is 1.67; the previous window's density was also 1.67, but that band has been amended, so this is
  an observation about this window, not a trend.
- **The costliest units are the review machinery's own artifacts.** Their recorded classes are a mix — the 43-round unit
  leads with `contradiction` and `claim-unreproducible` (14 each), then `coherence` 7, `wording` 7, `over-claim` 5, and
  `incomplete-enumeration` 4 — which the loop reports but does not mechanise.
- **Five window-archived change dirs carry six unticked boxes** — named in the table below with their dispositions.
- **A `check` run failed with no task name or message retained and did not reproduce** on two later runs; the diagnosis
  lost its evidence.
- **No artifact records a consultation's per-pass cost, or which passes changed nothing.** Their questions and reports
  are quoted in some change dirs, so what is missing is the per-pass record, not every trace of the consultations.

## Improvement suggestions

**P1 (priority) — `system`, accepted 2026-10-23 and recorded here: pause the program of prose-only consolidation for
now.**

- Pattern targeted: units whose product is moving or re-stating guidance, which dominated this window's composition and
  its round count. The always-loaded surface fell by a measured amount (`AGENTS.md` −505 lines) while the maintained
  corpus grew; whether further moves buy anything beyond the byte count is not established, and this report does not
  decide what the program's objective was.
- Expected observable outcome: the next window's units are chosen by a stated need rather than by the remaining parked
  moves; if a move is taken, its unit states which behavior or defect it remedies.
- Verification: a per-unit record of why the unit exists and what class of path it changes, read beside its rounds. The
  split this report attempted (guidance vs behavior units) is not used as a target until its criterion is defined.
- **This is a pause, not a prohibition.** Resumption is justified by a confirmed contradiction between owners, a
  retrieval failure in practice (a round or capture naming the rule it could not find), or another material process
  defect — not only by a behavior change that pulls a rule.
- **Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-23 as the current priority**, recorded here rather than as a new `docs/ideas.md`
  entry. The parked moves stay parked; the pause does not close the open items this report lists.
- Why there: it is a program decision with a revisit condition, not a rule an agent applies; it supersedes no
  requirement, so it needs no delta.

**P2 — `system`, unverified, now applied in `guard-archived-task-boxes`: a guard against an unticked box in a staged
archived change dir.**

- The window supplies five dirs and six boxes, with the dispositions above: three are one verbatim act, one is a
  bookkeeping box whose edit landed, one is a deferred ablation, and one is a completed control run whose box was not
  ticked. That is enough to keep the idea alive and not enough to design an exemption list — releasing a required task
  on its title alone would let a real omission pass.
- The design — which boxes are exempt, and how the guard reads the staged set without touching historical dirs — is its
  own change and its own review. This report does not propose starting it now.

**P3 — not proposed.** A per-consultation record would need a field, a delta, a description, and its own upkeep, and the
window does not establish that consultations are the highest-yield artifact — only that their per-pass record is
missing. The session-only context note is the current record; a new completion-criteria item is not justified yet.

**Not proposed:** a new rule for the miss classes (the claims-table mechanism is specified and the residual is not
established as a wording gap); a fixed round cap, diff-scoped follow-ups, or per-round telemetry (contradicted by the
2026-10-15 park); widening the 120-character check (already parked); a ledger wrapper (the contiguity net caught both
occurrences).

## Recommended next direction

**Pause the prose-only consolidation and choose the next unit by need.** The window does not support a stronger
conclusion: it cannot separate the loop's effect from the units it reviewed, it cannot compare its density with the
amended baseline, and its causal candidates are hypotheses. What it does support is a decision about _what to work on
next_: no parked move is urgent, the loaded surface has been reduced, and the two outstanding limitations (the
unreproduced `check` failure and the parked quick-review report gap — a report without a verdict or record line, whose
false-pass risk is untested) are recorded rather than actionable.
